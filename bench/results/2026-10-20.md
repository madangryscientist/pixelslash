# PixelSlash vs Argos, 2026-10-20

Two corpora, scored the same way for both tools: `bench/corpus/` (main), 72 pairs (37 real, 30 noise, 5 identical), and `bench/corpus-holdout/` (held out), 85 pairs (45 real, 36 noise, 4 identical). Tuning work looks at the main corpus only; the held-out one, made from pages the main corpus never sees, says whether a change helped beyond them. `bun run bench` reproduces this; `bench/README.md` says how it is measured.

- PixelSlash: `e42a336+dirty`, `diffPngs` at its defaults (perceptual threshold, anti-aliased edges set aside, a size mismatch is a change). Its regions are the engine's own (clustered at 24px, specks dropped, noise regions left out of the IoU); Argos's are extracted from its diff image at 8px (`src/regions.ts`).
- Argos: `21e8520c28129d501b22028a5499546b03336e38`, `diffImages` at threshold 0.5, answers from `bench/argos-cache.json` (VM 101 only for new or changed pairs, `--refresh-argos`). Its time is `diffImages` alone, as measured when the cache entry was made, without ssh, tar or container start.
