Nothing we have is unique

Every capability in the table exists somewhere else. Two are rare rather than unique: a local-only mode where nothing is uploaded (one other tool, and it is archived), and an AI judgement against design-system rasters (the others have AI that compares builds, not one that reads a design system). The diff's own accuracy is the other claim, and it is measured, not asserted: 35/37 real changes caught with 12/30 noise flagged, against Argos at 30/37 and 19/30 (bench/results/2026-10-20.md).

What is actually missing

The field, today

  • Lost Pixel is gone. The repository was archived on 22 April 2026; the README says it is joining Figma and sunsetting the product. Its column is a historical comparison, not a live competitor.
  • Argos is the closest thing to us: MIT-licensed, free hobby tier, deterministic diff, merge-base baselines, and the only one whose docs argue against AI. Its weakness is capture: it runs in your CI, so the renderer is whatever your runner has, and it has no diff-time ignore region.
  • Chromatic is the benchmark for the review loop (modes, diff inspector, checks, flake filter) and the one with the tightened free tier: 5,000 snapshots on Chrome only.
  • Percy (BrowserStack) sells cross-browser cloud rendering and Intelli Ignore; sensitivity is set at project or region level, not per snapshot.
  • Applitools has the deepest match-level surface (layout, content, strict, exact), accessibility checks, self-hosting via Private Cloud, and no free tier: the price is the opening for a cheap, honest diff.
