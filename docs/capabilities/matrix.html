Nothing we have is unique

Every capability in the table exists somewhere else. Two are rare rather than unique: a local-only mode where nothing is uploaded (one other tool, and it is archived), and an AI judgement against design-system rasters (Applitools checks a build against a Figma design and Percy's AI reviews changes against a PR summary; no docs page we read describes checking a design-system ruleset). The diff's own accuracy is the other claim, and it is measured, not asserted: 35/37 real changes caught with 12/30 noise flagged, against Argos at 30/37 and 19/30 (bench/results/2026-10-20.md).

What is actually missing

The field, today

  • Lost Pixel is gone. The repository was archived on 22 April 2026; the README says it is joining Figma and sunsetting the product. Its column is a historical comparison, not a live competitor.
  • Argos is the closest thing to us: MIT-licensed, a free hobby tier, merge-base baselines, and a diff its docs call deterministic, with no AI step. Its weakness is capture: it runs in your CI, so the renderer is whatever your runner has, and its ignore is per change fingerprint, not a region you draw.
  • Chromatic is the benchmark for the review loop (modes, diff inspector, checks, flake filter) and the one whose free plan is 5,000 snapshots a month.
  • Percy (BrowserStack) sells cross-browser cloud rendering and Intelli Ignore; sensitivity is set per project, per snapshot or per region, and its AI review is paid-plan only.
  • Applitools has the deepest match-level surface (a level per checkpoint or region, in place of a numeric threshold), accessibility checks, on-premises deployment sold as an add-on, and no free tier: the price is the opening for a cheap, honest diff.
